## Script 1 — Ship-night Reel (45s)
- 0–3s: Face — "I have 30 hours to make money with digital products."
- 3–10s: Screen — empty store folder → product list
- 10–25s: Fast cuts — markdown, PDF, HTML landing
- 25–35s: Phone — live store URL
- 35–45s: CTA — "Packs from $12. Link in bio / WhatsApp."

## Script 2 — Hook teardown (30s)
- 0–3s: Bad hook + red FAIL stamp
- 3–12s: Why it failed (vague, no promise)
- 12–22s: Rewrite with specific outcome
- 22–30s: "30 hooks in the pack — $12"

## Script 3 — M-Pesa friendly sell (40s)
- 0–3s: "Can't get Stripe yet? Sell anyway."
- 3–15s: WhatsApp + M-Pesa instructions card
- 15–30s: Deliver ZIP after payment screenshot
- 30–40s: CTA to store
